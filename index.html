I'm a Cybersecurity Engineering student at George Mason University (graduating 2027). I previously served as President of Cybersecurity GMU and President of AWS GMU, and I lead the Web Attack category for HackFax × PatriotHacks - Mason's largest CTF - where my team of 11 built 30+ challenges over six months for the biggest event the university has ever run.

By day I work as a Security Professional at a Tier III+ QTS Data Center campus, monitoring access control, running vulnerability checks, and triaging incidents. Before that I was a Network Specialist at Mindboard, building incident response playbooks, helping tune SIEM detections, and supporting NIST 800-53 / ISO 27001 controls. I picked up CompTIA Security+ and A+ along the way, and I'm a volunteer EMT with Arcola Volunteer Fire Department in Loudoun County's combined fire and rescue system. Eagle Scout, Troop 761.

tl;dr - I like building things, breaking things on purpose, and showing up when people need help.

PromptShield

A CLI + tiny web UI that scans LLM prompts for known injection patterns (instruction overrides, role-confusion, encoded payloads, indirect injection via tool output). Pattern library + heuristics + a small classifier. Built for AppSec teams shipping LLM features.

HoneyJar

A lightweight honeypot you can drop on a $5 VPS. Listens on commonly-probed ports (22, 23, 80, 445), logs attacker IPs, captured commands, and credential attempts, then ships structured events to a webhook (Discord / Slack / SIEM). Inspired by my SIEM tuning work at Mindboard.
